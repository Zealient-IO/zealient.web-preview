About

One partner instead of three.

Zealient is an AI-enabled tech services company for small and medium businesses in the Philippines and clients offshore.

Getting a product built usually means hiring an agency for the app, a DevOps contractor for the hosting, and an AI consultant for anything smart. Each one hands off to the next, and the business owner is left to hold it together.

Zealient does all three as one team. We build your web and mobile apps, run them on secure AWS infrastructure, and add AI where it solves a real problem. You get one conversation, one plan and one team responsible for the result.

We keep things practical: the simplest setup that does the job, running costs you can see, and security built in from the first release.
