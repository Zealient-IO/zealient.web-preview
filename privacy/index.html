Privacy

Privacy notice.

This notice explains what personal data this website collects and how Zealient uses it, in line with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

What we collect

When you send the contact form, we collect the details you enter:

  • Your name and email address
  • Your company name, if you give it
  • The service you are interested in and your message

Why we collect it

We use these details only to reply to your inquiry and to prepare a quote. We rely on the consent you give when you send the form.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your data. The form is delivered through Amazon Web Services, which processes the message on our behalf.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry details for as long as needed to handle your request and any work that follows from it, then delete them.

Your rights

You have the right to be informed, to access, correct or erase your personal data, to object to its processing, and to withdraw your consent. You may also file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission.

Contact

To use any of these rights, send us a message through the contact page.
